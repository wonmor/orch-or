Support — Orch-OR Visualizer

Orch-OR Visualizer is an interactive teaching model of the Penrose–Hameroff "Orchestrated Objective Reduction" theory of consciousness.

Questions, bugs, or feature requests: email wonmor@yahoo.com.

Frequently asked

  • Is this a real physics simulation? No. The lattice, the spread of coherence and the collapse rule ∫E_G dt ≥ ħ follow the theory's description, calibrated to Hameroff & Penrose (2014), but the recruitment rates and post-collapse states are illustrative.
  • Does the app endorse Orch-OR? No. The Learn tab presents the claim, the decoherence objection and the current evidence, with references.
  • Does anything leave my device? No. The app has no network access. See PRIVACY.md.
